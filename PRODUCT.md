# Titan CC — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Titan CC is a transcription web app for the SoExcellence team in India. Users upload MP4/audio files, a GPU worker (RunPod) transcribes them via Whisper, and the user downloads JSON, SRT, or TXT transcripts. Volume is low (8–9 jobs/month) but the tool must feel reliable and professional.

## Users
Internal SoExcellence content and research team. Professional, office-based, working in English and Indian languages. They use this on laptops during the day. They care about getting clean transcripts quickly and knowing when something goes wrong.

## Register
product

## Brand
SoExcellence (SoE) A&H brand identity.
- Primary: A&H Blue #00AEEF, Dark Blue #0076A3
- Magenta: Legacy Magenta #EC008C
- Accent greens, yellows, teals from the SoE palette
- Neutrals: White Grey #E6E7E8, Light Grey #B1B3B6, Dark Grey #1A1A1A
- Font: Gotham (Book 400, Medium 500, Bold 700, Black 800)

## Tone
Clear, professional, no-fluff. This is an internal tool — efficiency over decoration. Status should be immediately readable. Error messages should be actionable, not alarming.

## Anti-references
- No dark theme (office daylight use)
- No glass/blur decorations
- No gradient text
- No identical card grids
- No hero-metric layouts

## Strategic principles
- Status legibility above all: a user glancing at the Jobs list must instantly know what's in flight
- Every failed job must explain why it failed and what to do
- The upload flow must be forgiving (drag/drop, clear file size/format guidance)
